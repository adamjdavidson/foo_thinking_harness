# Mode image assets

The Boss, Buddha and Influencer tiles use AI-generated figurines of fictional people, made with Codex's built-in image generation tool. Play and Extract use CSS-rendered symbols, not image files. To personalize a tile, replace its file here with any square PNG.

- `boss.png`: a generic businesswoman bust.
- `buddha.png`: a jade Buddha figurine.
- `influencer.png`: a generic fashion model / social media influencer bust.

## Prompts

All three share this style brief: square icon for a pressable 3D mode tile in a warm ivory and forest-green thinking app. Premium tactile sculpted collectible in satin ceramic (jade for the Buddha) with softly rounded forms, real volume and subtle gold details. One centered figure filling 80% of the square, recognizable at 80px. Seamless warm ivory studio background, soft upper-left key light, natural ambient occlusion and a soft grounding shadow. No text, letters, logo, watermark, frame or extra objects.

- **Boss:** a generic, fictional professional woman boss as a head-and-shoulders bust, confident, warm, slightly expectant expression, as if reviewing your work; neat shoulder-length hair, tailored blazer over a simple top, small earrings. Not any real or recognizable person.
- **Buddha:** a beautifully sculpted small Buddha figurine, seated in meditation, serene gentle expression, respectful depiction with curled hair and simple robes, front three-quarter view.
- **Influencer:** a generic, fictional fashion model / social media influencer as a head-and-shoulders bust. Strawberry-blonde hair in a sleek high ponytail, oversized cat-eye sunglasses pushed up on her head, a playful confident smile, holding a small smartphone beside her face as if posing for a selfie. Fully covered outfit: a structured high-neck forest-green top with an ivory cropped jacket, small gold hoop earrings. Not any real or recognizable person.
